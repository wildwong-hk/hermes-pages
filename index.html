What it does

Hermes connects to the operator’s own Google Account — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts — in order to carry out tasks that the operator explicitly requests.

Typical tasks include:

  • Searching mail
  • Summarising threads
  • Drafting replies
  • Organising documents

Scope of access

Access is limited strictly to the operator’s own account and to the actions the operator asks for. No data belonging to any other person is accessed, collected or stored.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes what is accessed, how it is stored, and how access can be revoked.
